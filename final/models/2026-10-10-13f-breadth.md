## Hypothesis and mechanism

Chen, Hong & Stein (2002, JFE), "Breadth of ownership and stock returns". Short sales are constrained, so
when fewer institutions hold a stock, more pessimists are sitting out. The price then reflects mostly the
optimists and is too high, and future returns are low. A rise in breadth predicts high returns.
**Factor `dbreadth`, sign +1.** The test is one-sided: a negative t is a KILL, and the flipped sign is not run.
The expected overlap with the composite's price and fundamental factors is low. The overlap is reported:
the median per-date Spearman with each icw5_seas factor and with `short_interest_days_to_cover`
(descriptive, not gated).

**Trial count.** New family "institutional ownership / 13F", trial 1, k = 1. No earlier 13F or
institutional test exists in this project (COO grep, 2026-10-10).

### Vintage semantics (COO addendum probe, `probe_vintage*.py`, `probe_unique.py`, `probe_cf.py`; labels-free)

- **fdate is not an SEC filing date.**
  - Every fdate and every rdate in 2005-09..2020-03 is a calendar quarter-end. fdate is the TR vintage
    stamp.
  - fdate < rdate: **0** of 216,144 `s34type1` rows.
  - fdate = rdate: 206,521 rows. fdate > rdate: 9,623 rows.
- **Vintages per (mgrno, rdate).**

  | vintages | (mgrno, rdate) pairs |
  |---|---|
  | 1 | 202,981 |
  | 2 | 1,049 |
  | 3 | 241 |
  | 4 | 2,488 |
  | 5 | 78 |

  In 316 pairs (177 + 67 + 72) the first vintage is later than rdate, which means a late first appearance.
- **TR's own carry-forwards.** The fdate > rdate rows are stale rows that TR carries forward (gap of 1, 2,
  3 and 4 quarters: 3,924, 2,881, 2,740 and 78 rows).
  - They run at about 115–360 managers per vintage through 2017Q3.
  - They are **absent from 2017Q4 on**, a TR regime break. In the same quarter raw filers jump from 4,192
    to 4,805 (+14.6%).
- `s34type1` is unique per (fdate, mgrno) across all rdates (0 duplicate keys). The holdings join is
  therefore unambiguous.
- **Identical-holdings carry-forwards inside first vintages (`probe_cf.py`).**
  - Some managers file a first-vintage {(cusip, shares)} multiset at q that is exactly identical to their
    first-vintage multiset at q−1: 16–120 per quarter, 0.7%–2.5% of managers present in both quarters.
    Over all quarters that is 3,133 manager-quarters.
  - 2,091 of them hold ≤ 2 positions. Only 136 hold more than 20.
  - Identical cusip sets with changed shares are 2–3 times more common, which is ordinary turnover.

**Point-in-time rule (Gate A, binding).** The WO's fallback clause applies, because fdate is a vintage
stamp rather than a filing date.
- A quarter-q value is built only from the **first vintage**: `s34type1` fdate = rdate = q. Later vintages
  are never used. That covers carried-forward rows and late filers first stamped at a later vintage.
- The value is usable on panel date t only if **q + 60 calendar days ≤ t**. The q* used at t is the latest
  such quarter, asserted row by row.
- Residual risk, stated: a filing made between day 60 and the next quarter-end that TR still stamps
  fdate = q cannot be detected. The SEC 13F deadline is 45 days, so this residual is small.

**Carried-forward rule (fixed now).** A manager-quarter (m, q) is CF-flagged if m's first-vintage multiset
{(cusip, shares)} at q is exactly identical to m's first-vintage multiset at q−1. CF-flagged
manager-quarters are removed at q: they are not filers and hold nothing at q. Most are 1–2-position
managers, so the rule costs little breadth information, and it stops a stale copy counting as "present in
both quarters".

## Factor definition (frozen; `build_dbreadth.py`)

- **Filers and holders.**
  - F_q = managers with ≥ 1 first-vintage holding (shares > 0) at q, after CF removal. M_q = |F_q|.
  - Holdings map cusip → permno at q through stocknames ncusip, namedt ≤ q ≤ nameenddt. An ncusip that
    maps to more than one permno at q is dropped.
- **Breadth change (CHS adjustment).**
  - B_q = F_q ∩ F_{q−1}, the managers that filed in both quarters.
  - N_q(p) = #{m ∈ B_q holding p at q}. N'_{q−1}(p) = #{m ∈ B_q holding p at q−1}.
  - **dbreadth_q(p) = (N_q(p) − N'_{q−1}(p)) / M_{q−1}**, where M_{q−1} is all filers at q−1.
  - dbreadth is defined only if p has a date-valid stocknames record at both quarter-ends and has at least
    one holder in q or q−1. Otherwise it is NaN.
  - M_{q−1} is constant within a panel date, so it does not move within-date ranks.
- **Manager-count trap rule (WO text, fixed now).** Quarter q is FLAGGED if raw first-vintage filers fall by
  more than 10% versus the preceding quarter: Mraw_q < 0.90 · Mraw_{q−1}.
  - Rises are not drops. The seasonal Q4 jumps (+5% to +15%, new filers at year-end) and the 2017Q4 TR break
    are not flagged.
  - Result: Mraw rises smoothly, 2,423 (2005Q4) → 5,495 (2019Q4), and **no quarter is flagged** by this rule.
- **Security-coverage trap rule (name-what-should-be-there, `probe_vanish.py`, fixed now).**
  - Quarter q is FLAGGED if more than **100** permnos meet both conditions:
    - they had ≥ 10 holders in B at q−1 and 0 at q;
    - CRSP still lists them (no dsedelist dlstdt on or before q + 45 days).
  - Per-quarter counts: typically 0–44. They are **229 in 2011Q1, 301 in 2015Q2 and 914 in 2017Q4**. These
    are TR security-coverage gaps in which whole stocks drop out of the file.
  - Those three quarters are flagged.
- **Effect of a flag.** A change that spans a flagged quarter (q flagged or q−1 flagged) is NaN. That
  affects changes ending 2011Q1, 2011Q2, 2015Q2, 2015Q3, 2017Q4 and 2018Q1: 1,131,812 grid rows, all NaN
  (asserted).
- **Link to the grid.** At panel date t: Sharadar ticker → permno through the WO-51 crosswalk, with
  valid_from ≤ t ≤ valid_to, best match_quality, then lowest permno. Then the (permno, q*) value.
- Factor file: `final/out/wrds_13f/cache/dbreadth_factor.parquet`, **sha256
  `fb1d4ddeebe2a4130d2abbdf2a8747a6f06480e5d7a697149801f1c2caeaf11a`**. The screen asserts it at load.
