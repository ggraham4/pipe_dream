# WO-34: where R252's 2020-26 gain came from, and a forward side ledger for it (2026-09-30)

Follow-up to WO-33 (`final/models/2026-09-30-rolling-weights.md`). WO-33's
pre-registered verdict stands: refitting the icw9_seas weights on a trailing
1-year window (R252) is a KILL in-era (2010-2019: -1.11%/yr vs the expanding
control, 0/40 offsets). On 2020-26 the same arm was +2.60%/yr (38/40), read as
hold-out read #13. Gabe, 2026-09-30: "If the 1 year did well it bears serious
consideration so lets not dismiss it."

This note does two things and changes nothing live:

- **Part A** explains the 2020-26 gain (descriptive, no new fit; these reads
  are part of hold-out read #13).
- **Part B** starts recording R252 on forward data as a side-ledger column,
  `icw9_r252`, with a review rule fixed here before the first record.

No live weight, live pick or app change. Not deployed to the live checkout.

## Part A: attribution

Code `final/src/rollweights/r252_attrib.py`, output
`final/out/rollweights/r252_attrib.json`. WO-33's harness, weight paths, books
(40 offsets, net 15 bp) and comparison function, unchanged.

**Reconcile (hard assert, both pass):** R252 - EXP = +2.603%/yr, 38/40 on
2020-01-02..2026-07-30; -1.109%/yr, 0/40 on 2010-2019. Both equal WO-33.

**Method.** "Swap-in" starts from the EXP weight path and replaces one
factor's weight column with R252's. "Swap-out" starts from R252 and puts one
factor back on EXP's path. Rows are rescaled to sum|w| = 1 after a swap; the
score divides by sum|w| anyway, so the rescale never changes a score.

The single swaps do not add up to +2.60: the nine swap-ins sum to +1.04
(interaction +1.56) and the nine swap-outs to +5.15. The gain is mostly a
joint effect of moving several weights at once, so no single factor "owns" it.

**Short interest, three ways:**

| variant | R252-variant minus EXP | offsets + | share of the +2.60 that SI accounts for |
|---|---|---|---|
| R252 as run | +2.60 | 38/40 | |
| EXP with R252's SI path (swap-in) | +0.33 | 31/40 | 13% |
| R252 with SI on EXP's path (swap-out) | +2.30 | 37/40 | 11% |
| R252 with SI held at the rule's floor (t = NaN, as the live model has it) | +1.17 (t 0.82) | 32/40 | 55% |

The two readings differ because EXP also weights short interest once its
data exists (mean SI weight 2020-26: EXP -0.042, R252 -0.083; live -0.0105).
The work order quotes the live floor as -0.008; that is icw10_io's value. The
live icw9_seas floor is -0.0105.

**Plain answer.** Mostly not short interest. With SI put back on the
expanding control's path, R252 still beats EXP by +2.30 of the +2.60 (37/40):
89% of the gain survives, and SI's own share of the R252-vs-EXP gap is 11-13%.
"Weighting SI as soon as its data existed" is common to both arms, because
EXP does it too. The rest is a joint re-weighting of several factors (single
swap-ins sum to only +1.04, swap-outs to +5.15), with the gross-profitability
down-weight (0.38 to 0.20) the largest single piece; that same move cost the
most in-era.

Separately, measured against the *live* model instead of EXP: holding SI at
the live floor cuts R252's edge to +1.17%/yr (t 0.82, 32/40), so 1.43 of the
2.60 points (55%) lean on SI carrying a real weight at all. That is the
WO-25-style point, which Gabe chose to judge forward. It is a statement about
the live floor, not about what separates R252 from EXP.

### Turnover and cost

| | 2010-2019 EXP | 2010-2019 R252 | 2020-26 EXP | 2020-26 R252 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| share of the book that is new each 40-day rebalance | 0.490 | 0.615 | 0.397 | 0.565 |

R252 turns the book over about 25-40% more and costs 0.12-0.16%/yr more. The
+2.60 is already net of that. Mean L1 weight change per refit is 0.344.

### Caveat on the weight rule

The live rule uses |t|: `w = sign * max(0.1, |t| - 1)`. On a 1-year window a
factor whose recent IC runs *against* its fixed sign still gets a large
weight in the fixed-sign direction. In 2020-26 the R252 t opposed the fixed
sign in 12% (gross profitability) to 46% (volatility) of refits; for short
interest 19%, with mean t -1.38, so SI was mostly weighted the way its data
pointed. The latest refits are an exception: at 2026-07-17 SI's t is +2.03
and its weight -0.19. A sign-aware rule is a different rule and would be a
new fit on the hold-out; it was not run. Flagged as a COO follow-up.

## Part B: forward side ledger `icw9_r252`

### B1. What is recorded

On every weekly record date of the other side ledgers (complete ISO weeks
only, v3's dates after 2026-09-24, same `recorded_late` and `incomplete_week`
handling as seas and io), `record_weekly.py` appends one record to
`final/out/reset2026/prediction_ledger_r252.csv`: v3's cap150 rows with
`icw8_score`, `icw9_seas_score` (identical to the seas ledger, diff 0
enforced), `icw9_r252_score`, its rank, and the refit used.

Code: `final/src/rollweights/r252_forward.py`. Version tag
`r252v1_wo33rule_2026-09-30`.

### B2. The weights (frozen)

WO-33's rule exactly: at each refit, t = NW(39) t of the per-date Spearman IC
over the 252 most recent matured label dates, embargo idx(d) + 41 <= idx(refit);
weights from the live rule; refits every 21 trading days on WO-33's calendar
(next ones: idx 4935 = 2026-08-17, 4956 = 2026-09-16, 4977, ...); weights held
until the next refit.

IC source:

- dates up to the **seam, 2026-07-13**: WO-33's own ICs, pinned verbatim in
  `final/out/rollweights/r252_ic_pinned.csv` (2022-01-03 onward, with the
  WO-33 calendar index). 2026-07-13 is the last date on which all nine WO-33
  ICs are finite.
- dates after the seam: recomputed from the live working panel on WO-33's
  frames (8 factors on `eligible_cap150_v1` and v1 tickers; `seas` on
  `eligible_cap150` with seas from `seas_live`). A matured date whose label
  coverage is under 90% raises, which skips the record.

The weight path is stored append-only in
`final/out/reset2026/r252_weight_path.csv`, written at record time. A stored
refit is never recomputed or overwritten. The first record will back-compute
the two refits already due (08-17, 09-16); both use only labels matured by
their refit date.

### B3. Checks

- **Selftest** (`r252_forward.py selftest`, `r252_forward_selftest.json`):
  the 41 WO-33 R252 refits covered by the pinned file reproduce with max
  |dw| = 0 and |dt| = 0 (tolerance 1e-9); the full 235-refit WO-33 path
  reproduces from WO-33's IC parquet, max |dw| = 0. Seam: live-recomputed ICs
  vs pinned on 2026-01-02..07-13, max |diff| 1.1e-5 (seas 0), and the 07-17
  weights are identical either way. Scorer on 2026-09-24: icw8 pairs with v3
  (1e-16), icw9_seas equals seas_forward (0), icw9_r252 equals an independent
  computation (1e-12).
- **Isolation** (`wo34_isolation_test.py`, `wo34_isolation_test.json`):
  12/12 modes PASS (baseline, happy, import, plan, pinned file missing, build, label coverage, record, half-written, guard-log failure, seas skipped, second run). In every r252 failure mode v3/ext/hedge/sue/seas/blend_seas/io are identical to the pre-WO-34 baseline (content hash with recorded_at removed, plus their record-log rows), rc 0 (rc 1 only for the half-written record, after all others recorded), main store unchanged.
- `record_weekly.py --plan`: rc 0.

Weights the first record would use (refit 2026-09-16): SI -0.270, seas
+0.364, days-to-filing -0.097, gross profitability +0.080, the rest at the
floor (0.038). Rank correlation with icw9_seas on 2026-09-24 is 0.42, and the
top-300 lists share 101 names: this is a materially different ranking, which
is what makes the forward comparison informative.

Note for whoever reads the 26-record review: at that refit the two largest
weights point against their own trailing IC. SI's 1-year t is +1.72 (fixed
sign -1) and seas's is -1.97 (fixed sign +1); gross profitability's is -1.21
(fixed sign +1). So the early records mostly test the |t| rule's behaviour
(large weight in the fixed-sign direction after a year of the factor failing),
not "follow what worked last year".

### B4. Hook

`final/src/reset2026/record_weekly.py` rule 10: r252 is imported in its own
try, runs after io, requires the seas record for the same date, and any
failure (import, plan, pinned file, build, label coverage, pairing, record)
skips only the r252 record and is logged to
`final/out/reset2026/ledger_r252_guard_log.csv`. It never stops another
ledger. A record half-written by a raising record step is reported at the end
with rc 1, after every other ledger has recorded (same as io).

### B5. Forward review rule (fixed now, before any record)

- **Primary.** Per matured weekly record: rank-IC(icw9_r252) minus
  rank-IC(icw9_seas) on the same cap150 rows, label
  `forward_return_tradable_40` (close[t+40] / open[t+1]). Counted records are
  on-time and complete-week only. Statistic = paired mean over counted
  matured records.
- **At 26 counted matured records:** mean > 0 means keep tracking and bring
  it to Gabe. Mean <= 0 means continue to the next check.
- **At 52 counted matured records:** mean <= 0 means drop the column.
- **Secondary (descriptive).** Book-level comparison of the two rankings at
  the greedy >= 40-trading-day counted dates (WO-10's counted-date rule).
- No live-weight or live-pick change follows from this ledger without a
  separate decision by Gabe.

`r252_forward.py score` and `status` implement the primary rule.

## WO-34b (2026-10-01): picks file and weekly scoring

Two contract gaps the app manager found in the rolling-weights tab. Both
outputs are tracking only. No live weight, live pick, or ledger rule changes.

**1. Picks file.** `r252_forward.write_picks()` writes
`final/out/current_signal_r252.csv` and `current_signal_r252_meta.json`.

- It follows `current_signal_composite.main()` step for step, by import:
  `W.working_cross_section` on the working panel's latest date (v2 universe
  rule), `eligible_cap150`, `seas_live.seas_asof` on the whole eligible
  cross-section with the Theoretical coverage floor,
  `ICW.compute_composite_ic_weighted`, `composite.pick_decile_volq`. Only the
  weights differ: the R252 weights in force on that date.
- CSV columns are the Theoretical file's: `ticker, sector, close, market_cap,
  volatility_60, composite_score, weight, seas`, sorted by weight descending.
- The meta has the Theoretical meta's keys in the same order, then one extra
  block `r252` (`refit_date`, `refit_idx`, `refit_in_weight_path`, window
  dates, full-precision weights, `picks_overlap_vs_icw9_seas`, `written_at`).
  `backtest_summary` carries no number, only pointers to the two docs.
- The weight path stays append-only through ledger records. If the picks date
  needs a refit that no record has stored yet, the picks use it and the meta
  says `refit_in_weight_path: false`.
- Both files are written through a temp file and a rename.

**2. Weekly scoring.** After every ledger has recorded in a non-dry run,
`record_weekly.py` calls `r252_forward.score()` and then
`r252_forward.write_picks()`, each in its own try. A failure is a logged skip
in `ledger_r252_guard_log.csv` (event `r252_score_skipped` or
`r252_picks_skipped`, empty panel_date), never a STOP. Both run on every
non-dry run, not only in weeks with a new r252 record, because records mature
between records. `prediction_ledger_r252_scores.csv` columns: `n_names,
rho_icw9_r252, rho_icw9_seas, gain, panel_date, scored_at, version,
refit_idx` (`gain` = the paired difference). It first appears when the first
r252 record matures (41 trading days after it).

Standalone: `r252_forward.py picks` writes the picks files;
`r252_forward.py weekly` runs score then picks.
