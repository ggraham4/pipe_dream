## Hypothesis and mechanism

Informed traders with bad news buy OTM puts, which steepens the implied-volatility smirk before
the news reaches the stock (Xing, Zhang & Zhao 2010, JFQA: high-smirk stocks underperform low-smirk
stocks by ~10.9%/yr, persisting ≥ 6 months). Stocks with a steep smirk (OTM put IV − ATM call IV)
should underperform over the next 40 trading days. **Composite sign −1** (score = −smirk; low
smirk = good). One-sided: a raw IC t ≥ 0 (wrong sign) is a KILL and the flipped sign is not run.

**Scope fence.** Not tested here (dead or separate orders): opt_cw_spread in the long book (WO-36),
CSP, long calls, LEAPS, IV innovations (An-Ang-Bali-Cakici 2014), VRP.

**Trial count.** Options family trial **23** (29 including WO-36), per COO.md: options tally 22
after WO-52 (COO.md WO-52 entry, "Options tally 22 (28 incl WO-36)") and WO-55 BLOCKED before its
read (COO.md WO-55 entry, "options tally stays 22"). The family is past 20 trials, hence the raised
g1 bar (t ≥ 3.0) and the D-NWT null-t report.

## Factor definition (frozen)

- **smirk(i, t) = IV(put, Δ −20, 30d) − IV(call, Δ +50, 30d)** from vsurfd on surface date
  **s(t) = the latest OptionMetrics surface date strictly before panel date t** (global surface
  date set, i.e. the prior close), with **t − s(t) ≤ 5 calendar days** (else NaN; never binds in
  the era: observed 1–5 days). Either leg missing or NULL → NaN. **No fill**: if vsurfd lacks the
  name on s(t), smirk is NaN (no fallback to an older date, no opprcd fill). No winsorisation (the
  screen uses within-date ranks).
- **Link** (WO-52 rule): Sharadar ticker → permno at **t** (WO-51 crosswalk, valid_from ≤ t ≤
  valid_to; best match_quality, then lowest permno); permno → secid at **s(t)** (opcrsphist sdate ≤
  s ≤ edate or open; **lowest score, any score**, then a secid with a surface row on s, then lowest
  secid). Finite rows by link score: score 1 8,366,916, score 4 197. 218 finite rows share a secid
  with another ticker on the same date (kept; 0.003%).
- **PIT.** Surface date < t strictly (prior close), asserted row by row in the build. The label
  `forward_return_tradable_40` starts at open[t+1], so the factor has a ≥ 1-day lag.
- **Missing values**, same as rev3 in WO-53: NaN smirk → NaN rank-z; in the composite score
  (`signcheck.score`) a NaN factor contributes nothing and its |w| leaves the denominator (the
  score is the weighted mean over the factors present). In the IC, rows with NaN smirk drop out.

  audit/model_audit_wo23). No 2020+ outcome read; loaders assert max date < 2020-01-01.
- ★ **IC universe = cap2000** (WO-56: "cap2000 for the IC, cap150 for the book"). Gates 1–6 run on

  (same gate stack) is reported as descriptive only, not gated.
- **Base: icw5_seas** (`PRODUCTION_WEIGHTS_V5_SEAS`, the live composite). Before any book number,
  icw5_seas must reconcile to the WO-48b D4 arm (`arm_mean40` = 0.036442521084825354) to 1e-10, and
  the icw9_seas base is re-asserted by `dropcheck.base_chain`. Fail → stop and report.
- **ADMIT requires ALL of** (signs below are for SIGN = −1, i.e. applied to −smirk):
  1. ★ Pooled per-date Spearman IC (cap2000), Newey-West lag 39: **SIGN·t ≥ 3.0**. D-NWT: the NW t
     of 100 within-date permutations of smirk (seeds 56000..56099, cap2000) is computed; its sd and
     the one-sided empirical p of the real t are reported beside the NW t (reported, not gated).
  2. Odd-year and even-year halves both have SIGN·mean IC > 0.
  3. Both-sides sector-demeaned IC SIGN·t ≥ +1.0 (factor-only demeaning reported, not gated).
  4. 0/40 grid-offset sign flips (offset means of the daily IC, o = 0..39, all with sign SIGN).
  5. LOYO: SIGN·total summed IC > 0, max single-year share ≤ 45%, every leave-one-year-out SIGN·t > 0.
  6. Momentum residual (WO-50 `ind_mom_resid` construction): per-date cross-sectional OLS with
     intercept of raw smirk on raw momentum_12_1 (rows of that date where both are finite); the
     NW(39) IC SIGN·t of the residual ≥ +1.0. Median per-date Spearman with momentum_12_1 reported.
  7. Book (cap150): icw6 = icw5_seas + smirk vs icw5_seas, decile_volq, net 15 bp vs SPY, 40 offsets.
     - The five V5 weights are byte-exact. `w_smirk = SIGN · max(0.1, |t| − 1) · k`,
       `k = Σ|V5| / Σ max(0.1, |t_k| − 1)` over the V5 factors (t_k from
       `ic_weighted_composite_report.json` plus `SEAS_T`; each V5 weight asserted to 2e-4).
       ★ **t = the cap150 NW t of smirk** (same universe as the cap150 t_k the rule scales against;
       using the cap2000 gate-1 t would mix universes). In-sample weight (stated).
     - Paired increment = mean over 40 offsets of (icw6 − icw5).
     - Null: 100 draws, seeds 56000..56099, within-date permutation of the finite cap150 smirk
       values, re-ranked and given the **SAME w_smirk as the real factor**. Null increment =
       shuffled-icw6 − icw5, mean over offsets.
     - ★ Pass: paired increment > null p80 (numpy linear) **and ≥ 24/40 offsets positive**. Base
       sd40 reported, not the bar. Null draw 0 rerun must be bit-identical.
     - Book Δ and per-offset counts are reported whatever the gate 1–6 outcome.
